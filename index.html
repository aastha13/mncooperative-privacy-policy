Privacy Policy

How M.N. Cooperative Urban Thrift & Credit Society Limited handles personal information in connection with its website, society records and member communications.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to the Society’s public website and to information handled for membership and society services, including records maintained in the Society’s management software. The Society is responsible for handling member information described here.

The public website currently provides general information and contact details; it does not provide member sign-in or account forms.

Information we handle

Depending on the service and records provided, the Society may handle:

  • Identity and contact details, such as name, address, phone number, email address and membership number.
  • Membership, share, savings, fixed-deposit, loan, repayment, receipt and other accounting records.
  • Information supplied for applications and verification, which may include PAN, Aadhaar, photographs, bank details, supporting documents and guarantor or witness details.
  • Communications with the Society and information needed to prepare statements, receipts, certificates and other society documents.
  • For WhatsApp communications, the phone number used, message or document details, and available sending, delivery or read-status information.
  • Basic technical request information that the website hosting provider may record when someone visits the public website, such as browser, device, time and network information.

How we use information

The Society uses information to administer membership and society accounts; process savings, deposits, loans, repayments and accounting; verify applications; prepare and provide records or documents; communicate service updates and payment reminders; protect the accuracy and security of records; and meet applicable audit, regulatory and legal obligations.

WhatsApp may be used to send receipts, society documents and service reminders to the phone number recorded for a member. The Society may keep message history and status information to track whether a message was accepted, delivered or read when those status updates are made available.

When information is shared

Information is made available only as needed for society operations and the purposes above. This may include authorized Society personnel; Meta and WhatsApp when the Society sends or receives WhatsApp communications; providers that host or support the Society’s website, software or messaging delivery; and auditors, regulators, courts or public authorities where required for lawful society administration.

WhatsApp and its service providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. A message or document sent through WhatsApp is handled by those services as part of delivery.

Storage, security and retention

Society records are maintained in the Society’s management systems and in the records used to operate the Society. Access is intended for authorized personnel who need the information for their duties. Website and messaging services may also process the information needed to provide those services.

The Society keeps records for as long as needed to provide services, maintain accurate accounts, handle disputes and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Retention periods can vary by record type. When information is no longer required, the Society will take steps to delete or securely dispose of it where appropriate and legally permitted.

Your requests and choices

You may contact the Society to ask about personal information associated with your membership, request correction of inaccurate details, or request deletion where applicable. The Society may need to verify your identity and may retain records that it is required to keep for accounting, audit, regulatory or legal purposes.

To make a privacy or deletion request, call or visit the Society using the contact details below. Please do not send sensitive identity documents through an unsecured channel unless the Society has specifically instructed you to do so.
